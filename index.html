Asked across the counter

Questions owners ask.

Will callers know they're talking to an AI?

Yes. It answers with your shop's name, says it's an AI and that the call may be recorded, then asks how it can help. It never pretends to be a person.

Will my customers hate it?

It only picks up the calls you miss, so your regulars still reach you the way they do now. Instead of a full voicemail box, they get your shop's name, an honest "I'm an AI," and someone writing down what they need. Call Bella at 541-833-0643 and judge for yourself.

What if someone asks for a price?

It won't make one up. It takes the caller's name, number and what they need, so you call back with the real answer.

How do I get the message?

By email, right after the call: name, number (read back to the caller), where they are, what they need, and when to call back. Urgent calls say URGENT up front. Text alerts are coming soon. Want to see one first? Call the demo line and ask, and Bella can email you a sample summary. Yours start once your shop is set up.

Is recording calls legal?

It depends on the state. Bella announces that the call may be recorded on every call, so that consent is asked for up front. This is not legal advice; check with your own advisor if you're unsure.

Do I keep my phone number?

Yes. I set up the forwarding on your phone with you, and you can turn it off anytime.

Can I pause it for the slow season?

Yes, free, for up to 3 months a year. One text or email starts it, your calls forward straight back to your shop, and there's no restart fee.
