What if someone asks for a price?

It won't make one up. It never quotes prices. It takes the caller's name, number and what they need, so you call back with the real answer.

What happens to the message?

You get an email summary of each call with the caller's name, number (read back to them), where they are and what they need, so you can call them back.

Is recording calls legal in Oregon?

For phone calls, Oregon generally allows recording when one party consents (ORS 165.540), and the AI announces the possible recording on every call anyway. This is not legal advice; check with your own advisor if you're unsure.

Do I keep my phone number?

Yes. I sit down with you and set up the forwarding on your phone myself, and you can turn it off anytime.
