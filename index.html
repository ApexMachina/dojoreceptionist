Questions shop owners ask me

Will callers know they're talking to an AI?

Yes. I set it up so it tells every caller it's an AI assistant and that the call may be recorded. It never quotes prices and never books a job without you.

Is recording calls legal in Oregon?

For phone calls, Oregon generally allows recording when one party consents (ORS 165.540), and the AI announces the possible recording on every call anyway. This is not legal advice; check with your own advisor if you're unsure.

Do I keep my phone number?

Yes. Nothing changes for your customers. I walk you through the forwarding myself, and you can turn it off anytime.
