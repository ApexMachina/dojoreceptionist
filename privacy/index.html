Privacy

What happens to a call.

Plain English. Bella takes a message so the shop can call you back. That's all your details are used for.

Last updated November 10, 2026

When you call a shop that uses Bella

  • Bella answers the calls the shop misses.She says she's an AI at the start of every call.
  • Calls may be recorded and transcribed.Bella says so at the start of every call. The recording and transcript are used to take your message accurately and deliver it to the shop.
  • Your details go to the business owner.Your name, your callback number, what you need and when to call back are emailed to the owner of the shop you called, along with the call transcript, so they can call you back.
  • Bella doesn't take payment.She never asks for card numbers, so don't give one on the call.

Who else handles it

  • We don't sell your data.Not to advertisers, not to list brokers, not to anyone.
  • The services that make the call work.Bella's voice and call handling run on ElevenLabs, our voice AI provider. Calls reach Bella through our phone carrier. The summary email is delivered by our email service. They process call data to provide that service to us.
  • The demo line and web demos.Calls to 541-833-0643 and the "Hear it" demos on this site are handled by ElevenLabs in the same way. Demo calls don't send a summary to any shop.

How long we keep it

  • Only as long as we need it.We keep call recordings, transcripts and summaries only as long as needed to deliver the message and support the shop's service. Once a summary email is sent, the shop holds its own copy.
  • This website.This site uses no ads, no tracking pixels and no analytics. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs. The cost calculator runs in your browser, and nothing you type leaves the page.

Questions or a request

To ask what we hold about a call, or to ask us to delete it, email jeremy@dojoreceptionist.com. A person reads every email. If you called a particular shop, you can also ask that shop directly.
