How many calls did you miss this week?

It's a hard number to know when you're under a sink, on a ladder, or with a customer when it rings. Tally every missed call for one week, on paper or on your phone, then see a rough estimate of what that week could have been worth, by your own numbers.

What you get

  • A printable letter-size log: one page of tally boxes by day and time block, one page for callback notes and the week's math.
  • A phone version that adds up the week, shows your busiest day and time, and does the estimate (this week, and a month at that pace).
  • Nothing leaves your phone.

How it works

  1. One tally mark every time the phone rings and nobody picks up.
  2. Do it for seven days. Skip spam and robocalls.
  3. Enter your average job value and booking rate. Missed calls × booking rate × job value = your estimate for the week.

Every dollar figure comes from numbers you enter. It's your estimate, not a promise.
