What you earn

$50 per shop

Flat $50 per shop, paid after that shop's first paid month. One thank-you per shop you refer, with no limit on how many shops you send.

How a referral works

  1. You get a partner code, like BK-07, and a link: dojoreceptionist.com/?ref=BK-07
  2. The shop gets in touch by calling the demo line, emailing Jeremy, or using your link, and mentions your code.
  3. Jeremy sets Bella up, in person in Lane County. Founders shops get their first week free. Founders pricing: first 5 shops only.
  4. The shop's first paid month clears, and your $50 goes out in the next monthly payout, by PayPal or Venmo, once Jeremy approves it. Jeremy emails you either way.

No code mentioned? Tell Jeremy the shop's name within 30 days of their signup and he'll match it by hand.
