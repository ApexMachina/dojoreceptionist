Which one fits your shop

A live answering service is better if...

  • Your callers need long or complicated conversations, the kind that need a person to think on their feet.
  • You want calls transferred to you or your on-call tech live, not a callback.
  • You want jobs booked on your calendar or deposits taken during the call.
  • You want Spanish-speaking live agents (offered by Ruby and Smith.ai1,2).
  • Your customers won't talk to an AI at all.

Voicemail is enough if...

  • You get only a few calls you can't answer.
  • You check messages often and call back fast.
  • Most callers already know you and don't mind waiting.

Bella fits if...

  • You miss calls while you're on a job, under a sink, or after hours.
  • You want every caller answered, with a summary emailed to you, for $129 a month.
  • You'd rather nobody but you ever quotes a price or diagnoses a problem.
  • You want setup from a real person: Jeremy sets it up with you himself.

Questions

Is Bella a real person?

No. Bella is an AI phone receptionist. She tells every caller she's an AI and that the call may be recorded, then takes their details for your team.

Will Bella quote prices or book jobs?

No. Bella never quotes prices, diagnoses, books, or takes payment, and she does not transfer calls live. She takes the caller's name, number, and what they need, emails the owner a summary, and your team calls them back. If you want booking, payment, or a live transfer on the call, a live answering service does that and Bella doesn't.

What happens on an urgent call?

Bella takes the caller's details, flags the call urgent so your callback person sees it first, and emails you a summary. If a caller smells gas, she tells them to leave and call 911 or the gas company from outside. She does not transfer calls live.

Is there a contract or setup fee?

No contract and no setup fee. The first week is free, then it's $129 a month for up to 150 calls. Jeremy sets Bella up for you personally, or by phone.

When is a live answering service the better choice?

When your callers need long or complicated conversations, when you want calls transferred to you live, or when you want someone to book jobs or take deposits on the call. A live service does those things; Bella doesn't. If that's your shop, compare the plans in the table above and pick one.
