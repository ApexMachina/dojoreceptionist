The service

  • Bella answers the calls you miss.When you don't pick up or the line is busy, your calls forward to Bella. She answers with your shop's name, takes the caller's name, number and what they need, and emails you a summary right after the call.
  • You keep your number.Nothing is ported or replaced. Your calls forward to Bella only when you don't answer, and you can turn forwarding off anytime.
  • You stay in charge.You confirm every appointment, approve every job, and call your customers back yourself.

Three things Bella never does

  • Never quotes a price.Pushed for a number, she says you'll give the real one when you call back.
  • Never diagnoses.She writes down what the caller sees and hears. You decide what it is.
  • Never takes payment.No card numbers on the phone, ever.

AI and recording

  • Callers hear that she's an AI.Bella says she's an AI at the start of every call. She never pretends to be a person.
  • Calls may be recorded.Bella says the call may be recorded at the start of every call, and calls are transcribed so the message is accurate. Recording rules vary by state; this page isn't legal advice. See Privacy for how call details are handled.

Price and billing

  • $129 a month.One plan. No setup fee. No contract. No minimum.
  • First week free.Try it on your real calls for a week before your first charge.
  • 150 calls a month.Spam, robocalls and hang-ups under 15 seconds don't count.
  • No overage charges, ever.If you run over two months in a row, I'll call you to talk it through.
  • Month to month.There's no term to sign. Each month stands on its own.

Pausing and canceling

  • Free pause, up to 3 months a year.One text or email starts it. Your calls forward straight back to your shop, and there's no restart fee.
  • Cancel the same day.Email jeremy@dojoreceptionist.com and I'll turn it off that day, with no last-month charge.
