Where this comes from

When I worked at a leadership academy, we got together once a week and wrote down two lists: what worked, and where we were failing. And the failures were never the students'. They were ours. That one meeting made us better every single week.

Most shop owners never get that meeting. You're too busy doing the work to step back and look at it. So I bring it to you. It's not a dashboard to log into. It's a short debrief from a person, every Monday morning.

How it works

  1. Bella answers the call and emails you the summary, the way she already does.
  2. After the job, one tap sends a review ask. It's a short text, already written, from your own phone, with your Google review link. One reminder after 3 days, then it stops.
  3. New reviews come to you with a reply drafted. Approve it, change it, or skip it.
  4. Once a month, text me a photo. I'll post it to your Google profile with a short caption.
  5. Every Monday, your Huddle. 3 wins, 3 misses that are on us, 1 fix, and whether last week's fix got done.

Questions

Is this software?

No. A person writes your Huddle: me. The review ask goes out from your own phone, so customers see your name, not a stranger's number.

Do I need Bella?

The Huddle is built as an add-on to a Bella plan, because Bella's call summaries are half of what goes into it. If you want it on its own, email me.

What if I get a bad review?

It goes in the misses, with a calm reply drafted for you to approve. One honest reply to a bad review often tells the next customer more than ten 5-star ones.

How long does it take me each week?

About as long as it takes to read one email and do one fix.
